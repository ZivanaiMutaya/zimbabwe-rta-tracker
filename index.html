How this data is kept honest

  • One record per incident. If ZRP and a newspaper both report the same crash, it is one row with two sources.
  • Unknown stays blank, never a guessed zero. A blank fatality or injury count means "not reported".
  • Coverage log. Days and provinces actually monitored are logged separately, so "no accidents recorded" is never confused with "nobody was watching".
  • Confirmed only in the totals. Anything flagged unverified is kept out of the headline numbers.

This is a record of reported and confirmed accidents, not a claim to count every accident in Zimbabwe. Underreporting is real.
